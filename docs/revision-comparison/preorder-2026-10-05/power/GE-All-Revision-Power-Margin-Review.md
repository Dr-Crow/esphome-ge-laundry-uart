No schematic, board, BOM, firmware, purchasing, publication or physical test was changed by this analysis.

**Result:** useful analytical bounds are now available for all seven revisions. They do not close an appliance-specific source or physical qualification. Rev3C's preserved C3/C6 choices each have a different low-input and thermal budget. Rev2.0/2.1/2.2 have a genuine **200 mA 3V3 regulator capability mismatch** against the WROOM module's ≥500 mA supply requirement; their old protection and thermal constraints also prevent calling them qualified fallback boards. No justified single component substitution closes every gate.

## Numbered power and USB paths

The read-only [numbered-net audit](numbered-power-net-audit.json) independently compares native netlist pin groups to actual PCB pad groups in all seven revisions: **zero physical net-membership differences**. Four Rev3B NC names encode a slash differently (`/` versus `{slash}`); their single-pin membership is identical. This checks connectivity, not physical conductance, source capability or an energized board.

For current Rev3A/B/C:

- **PIN1:** J1.1 → F2.1/2 → Q3 drain3/source 2 → U9 VS8/OUT7 → D14 anode2/cathode1 → V_INPUT → U8 VIN3/EN2. **PIN3:** J1.3 → F1.1/2 → Q4 drain3/source 2 → U10 VS8/OUT7 → D15 anode2/cathode1 → same V_INPUT.
- U8 SW5 → L1.1/2 → BUCK_5V; U8 FB1 senses that output; BST6/C15 and GND4 retain their original functions. D11 anode2 at BUCK_5V and cathode1 at +5V isolates the buck output.
- Q3/Q4 gate1 follows its gate-bias node through 51 Ω. Zener D16/D17 cathode1 is at PMOS source 2; anode2 is at the 5.1 kΩ-to-GND bias node. U9 IN1 follows its own protected source. U10 IN1 is PIN3_EN, R36 pulls it from PIN3_PROTECTED and Q5 collector3 sinks it. Q5 base1 receives PIN1_PROTECTED through R35 with R37 to GND; emitter2 is GND.
- Rev3C J6.7=+5V/VBUS, J6.6=GND and J6.5=module 3V3 **output**. Rev3B U2.14=VBUS and U2.12=3V3. Do not externally drive the 3V3 output or battery pads.
- Rev3A USB J4 VBUS → F3 → D10 anode2/cathode1 → +5V → U6 VIN1/EN3 → U6 VOUT5 → WROOM U2.1. Its USB data/ESD paths do not imply power-source qualification. F3 is exact Littelfuse 1206L050YR: 6 V maximum differential, 0.5 A hold at 20 °C, R1max 0.7 Ω; reference hold is 0.31 A at 70 °C / 0.25 A at 85 °C. At the 366.66 mA proxy its room-R1max drop is 257 mV and dissipation94 mW, before D10. Hot USB continuous-current margin is open.

The preserved Q5 selector senses **PIN1 presence**, not useful power. If PIN1 is weak, faulting or starting in repeated switch retry, it can still inhibit healthy PIN3. That is retained behavior, not new automatic fault fallback.

## Protection, derating and fault energy

40 V TPS switches do **not** rate the carrier40 V. The weakest condition can be PMOS differential, PPTC differential, buck operating voltage, diode heat, capacitor or resistor temperature, rather than a switch's own VS rating.

- **Gate network:** at 26 V source/nominal 12 V zener/−1%5.1 kΩ, resistor power38.82 mW, zener≈33.27 mW. The exactMCC voltage11.4–12.7 V is specified at 5 mA/25 °C; actual≈2.8 mA is below that test. Its typical temperature coefficient and impedance do not produce a guaranteed fast/hot VGS bound. Zener steady power is ≤Vsource²/(4Rmin)=33.47 mW for any split at 26 V, but this says nothing about transients. At40 V a nominal 12 V split gives155.3 mW in125 mW R31/R32: another reason40 V cannot be claimed. R35/R36 at 26 V dissipate64.7/67.8 mW with the stated base/collector assumptions; a125°C0805 derated reference is44.1 mW, while85 °C is102.9 mW. See [conditional-derating.csv](conditional-derating.csv);125 °C is a local-component fault/hot screen, not a supported whole-board ambient.
- **PMOS:** CJ3407 is−30 V VDS/±20 V VGS. Charged protected source 26 V followed by drain−13.6 V demands39.6 V differential, beyond rating. The current manufacturer's96.2 °C/W/1.3 W reference requires 1×1 inch FR4, high-coverage2 oz double-sided copper andt≤10 s. It cannot replace the actual board's steady thermal evidence. Neither a reverse-step occurrence nor surviving waveform has been observed here.
- **Diodes:** current manufacturer-authoredMCC MBR0540 sheet Rev3-4-12012020 specifies operatingTJ≤125 °C,0.5 A atTA50 °C and244 °C/W on its specified FR4 lands. At356.66 mA×0.55 V=196 mW, that reference rise47.9 °C reaches133 °C at 85 °C ambient. This is a mixed-condition upper-drop screen, not an observed fault or an all-temperature VF guarantee. At13.6 V the converted current proxy162.56 mA gives89.4 mW/21.8 °C rise. Current-primary rating and exact lot evidence must govern; older150 °C sheets cannot silently supersede it. PMEG2010ER's20 V reverse and0.34 V@1 A/25 °C limits remain independent; reverse leakage/thermal runaway need checking.
- **TVS and fuse:** SUNMATE SMF16A has16 V stand-off,17.8–19.7 V breakdown at 1 mA,26 V clamp at 7.7 A/10/1000 µs/25 °C,200 W for that pulse and385 mW DC under its stated mounting. Its printedDC derating4 mW/°C and325 °C/W reference must not be treated as a universal sustained shunt rating. The switch current limiter is **downstream** of the TVS. An illustrative8 A×26 V×0.20 s=41.6 J fault demand is not a permissibleTVS energy. The PPTC0.20 s trip reference at 8 A is not a survival coordination proof. Repetition, source impedance, current, overshoot and fault duration remain unknown.
- **Retry/hard short:** max partialCL704 mA×26 V×45 ms gives0.824 J electrical demand in one switch attempt, or0.431 J at 13.6 V. Thermal interruption can change it. The5.3% maximum partial retry duty is not a junction-temperature guarantee, and the40 mJ inductive turn-off energy rating is a different test. Unloaded510 mA×35 ms=17.85 mC charge budget excludes converter, RF and all startup loads.
- **Capacitors:** C9/C10 are50 V at V_INPUT, C20/C21 are50 V at protected nodes; at 26 V their nominal voltage utilization is52%. C18/C19 see gate-source differential, not raw source-to-GND voltage. C16/C17 and the low rails are25 V; they do not improve the upstream PMOS/buck limits. Nominal stored energy: C9 10 µF at 26 V=3.38 mJ; each1 µF protected-node cap=0.338 mJ;44 µF buck output at 5 V=0.550 mJ. ## Bounded next actions

1. **Now, without changing hardware:** preserve architecture and history; update/retain exact current-primary ratings, the actual module conversion paths, declared rail domains and the200 mA legacy capability blocker. Correct a stale regulator alias only as a separately verified numbered-pin metadata correction. Keep current and voltage scenarios visibly conditional.
2. **Legacy rated-capability repair needs a decision:** a new Rev2 candidate requires a verified continuous3V3 supply that supports Espressif's≥500 mA module provision with a separately allocated carrier-current allowance, plus appropriate dropout, thermals, reverse bias and numerical-pin compatibility. A typical500 mA short-circuit limit is insufficient. No exact drop-in substitute is established by this review. Merely changing the BOM to a larger number, enlarging the fuse or suppressing brownout does not close the issue.
3. **Rev3 local component options remain conditional:** if the agreed OEM/temperature/load envelope requires more D14/D15 current or less drop than the exact part supplies, review a higher-thermal/lower-drop diode with the same two original OR functions and verified footprint/polarity. If sustained high-source or local temperature requires moreR31/R32/R35/R36 dissipation, review higher-power exact resistors with retained values/functions. These are bounded candidates for review; no costed exact substitute or mandatory repair follows from an unknown envelope.
4. **No source narrowing to force a pass:** retain intended nominal 5/7.5/9/13.6 V cases. Obtain OEM supply tolerance, allowance, impedance and measured positive/negative waveforms, then decide acceptance criteria and any necessary repair. Require controlled startup/RF/brownout/handoff/USB/backfeed/thermal and source-energy evidence on actual hardware before compatibility or manufacturing claims.
