# Rev3B source-referenced gate protection

Q3/Q4 are retained CJ3407 PMOS devices. Their drain pin3 now connects to the respective PIN*_FUSED node, and source pin2 to PIN*_PROTECTED. The body diode permits positive-input charging before channel turn-on; at initially unpowered output it blocks negative drain input. Transition/precharged reverse-current behavior remains unqualified.

D16/D17 are exact MCC BZT52C12-TP / C668891: cathode pin1 goes to source/PIN*_PROTECTED; anode pin2 to gate-bias/PIN*_GATE_CLAMP. Retained R31/R32 5.1 kΩ pull bias toward GND. Retained C18/C19 100 pF/50 V C0G connect source to bias, and retained R33/R34 51 Ω connect bias to actual gate. No extra source-to-gate DC pull-up is introduced.

The [MCC primary sheet Rev3-4-12012020](https://www.mouser.com/datasheet/2/258/BZT52C2V4_7eBZT52C75_500mW__SOD_123_-2904721.pdf) specifies 11.4–12.7 V at 5 mA/25°C, and ≤0.1 µA leakage at 8 V. It permits maximum differential impedance 25 Ω at5 mA/150 Ω at1 mA, compared with [former Nexperia BZT52](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/BZT52_SER.pdf)'s10/90 Ω. MCC +6…+10 mV/K is explicitly typical at unspecified IZTC; no comparable capacitance or surge bound is given. Nexperia's coefficient wording or ≤85 pF cannot be inherited. MCC is the reviewed selection, not an assertion of dynamic equivalence.

MCC's suggested pads are0.91 ×1.22 mm,2.36 mm inner gap,4.18 mm overall span. Retained SOD-123 pads are0.90 ×1.20 mm at±1.65 mm, giving2.40 mm inner gap/4.20 mm span. Its3.55–3.85 mm total length,2.55–2.85 mm body length,1.40–1.70 mm width and≤1.35 mm height fit the retained envelope. Marking is WH; cathode band faces pad1. Polarity, placement tolerance and solder acceptance remain assembly review gates.

At illustrative26 V source/12 V clamp, 5.1 kΩ bias carries2.745 mA; zener dissipation is32.9 mW and pull-down dissipation38.4 mW. This is below5 mA voltage characterization, so11.4–12.7 V is not a guaranteed actual-bias clamp range. Neglecting gate DC current, steady diode power is bounded by V²/(4R),33.14 mW nominal/33.47 mW at−1% resistor tolerance. This does not bound VGS or transient energy. MCC500 mW at25°C derates linearly to zero at150°C, giving100 mW at125°C under its stated conditions;250°C/W gives indicative8.3°C rise at33 mW with mounting conditions unspecified. No board thermal upgrade follows.

Low-current/hot clamp behavior, Miller coupling, gate-bias capacitance, actual fast gate/source peaks, charged reverse steps and both branches' temperatures remain qualification gates.
