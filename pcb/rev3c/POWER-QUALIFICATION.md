This selected prototype adds the [rated-switch protection delta](RATED-SWITCH-OPTION.md) to frozen gate-corrected source 8f95555. It preserves both original input branches, automatic PIN1-presence priority, common AP63205 buck and C3/C6 interfaces. The40 V switch is a component choice for a bounded protection review; no observed OEM failure,40 V carrier rating, mandatory upgrade or manufacturing approval is established.

PIN1 remains J1.1→F2→Q3→U9→D14→V_INPUT. PIN3 remains J1.3→F1→Q4→U10→D15→V_INPUT. PIN1_PROTECTED still drives Q5 through R35/R37; Q5 pulls PIN3_EN low. With PIN1 absent, R36 pulls U10 IN high from its own protected source. Q5 senses presence rather than useful output, so weak/faulting PIN1 can inhibit healthy PIN3. This preserved behavior is not a new fault-aware fallback.
