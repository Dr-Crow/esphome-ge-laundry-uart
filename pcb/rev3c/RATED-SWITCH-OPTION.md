The electrical source reviewed and quoted is `7bb455fbc85c748f3125ed091a54375de69fbbcc`; its unchanged hardware remains a prototype with open source, startup, thermal, assembly and physical gates. ## Reviewed gate-clamp sourcing correction

The [MCC-authored BZT52 sheet, Rev.3-4-12012020](https://www.mouser.com/datasheet/2/258/BZT52C2V4_7eBZT52C75_500mW__SOD_123_-2904721.pdf), pp1–3, supports the nominal voltage and package/polarity choice. Pin1 remains cathode at the respective protected PMOS source; pin2 remains anode at its gate-bias node. MCC's suggested pads are 0.91 ×1.22 mm with 2.36 mm inner gap and 4.18 mm overall span. Existing pads are 0.90 ×1.20 mm at ±1.65 mm, giving 2.40 mm gap and 4.20 mm overall span. Its 3.55–3.85 mm total length, 2.55–2.85 mm body length, 1.40–1.70 mm width and ≤1.35 mm height fit the existing SOD-123 envelope. No footprint edit is made; assembler polarity, placement tolerances and solder acceptance still require review. MCC marking is **WH**, with the cathode band facing pad1.

Both MCC and the [former Nexperia sheet](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/BZT52_SER.pdf) specify 11.4–12.7 V at 5 mA/25°C and ≤0.1 µA leakage at 8 V. MCC permits **25 Ω at 5 mA /150 Ω at 1 mA**, versus Nexperia's 10 Ω /90 Ω. That permits a softer clamp. MCC's +6…+10 mV/K is explicitly **typical**, at an unspecified IZTC, and its sheet gives no comparable capacitance or surge rating. Nexperia's ≤85 pF and bounded temperature-coefficient wording cannot be inherited. This is a reviewed sourcing alternative, not a claim of identical dynamic behavior at every corner.

At an illustrative 26 V source and 12 V clamp, the retained 5.1 kΩ bias carries about2.745 mA and the zener dissipates about32.9 mW. That current is below the 5 mA voltage characterization: **11.4–12.7 V is not a guaranteed clamp range at actual bias**. Neglecting gate DC current, steady dissipation at 26 V is bounded by V²/(4R), about33.14 mW nominal or33.47 mW at −1% resistor tolerance; this does not bound VGS or transient energy. MCC's 500 mW at25°C derates linearly to zero at150°C, giving100 mW at125°C under the sheet's conditions. Its250°C/W implies an indicative8.3°C rise at33 mW, with mounting conditions unspecified. No board thermal upgrade is established. Low-current/hot clamp, actual gate-source fast peaks, Miller coupling, reverse steps and both branches' thermal behavior remain qualification gates. Neither the 26 V illustration nor a nominal12 V label expands the allowed source envelope.

The [corrected C149504 supplier identity](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C149504) retains 100 kΩ ±1%,125 mW,150 V,±100 ppm/K and −55…155°C in0805. ## Current, startup and loss costs

The resistor sets nominal ICL = 2000/3300 = **0.606 A**. Combining TI's conditional ±15% with resistor ±1% gives approximately **0.510–0.704 A**. Accuracy requires VS−OUT ≥2.5 V and the stated setting range. Including ±100 ppm/K resistor drift from 25°C widens that estimate to about 0.507–0.708 A at 85°C or 0.504–0.713 A at 150°C. This is a provisional engineering current budget, not an OEM current allowance or unconditional draw ceiling. CL dissipation at its nominal 0.8 V is about 0.194 mW; the resistor is 100 mW through 70°C, derating to zero at 155°C.

TPS1H200 replaces the old CT-controlled startup with its internal slew and current limiting. Continued current limit yields **35–45 ms on / 0.8–1.2 s off** (40 ms/1 s typical). Those timing values are specified by design, not production tested; thermal interruption can affect the observed attempt. A load that cannot finish startup in an attempt can repeatedly reset. The unloaded illustrative minimum charge budget, 0.510 A ×35 ms≈17.9 mC, does not include buck or module demand. The retained switch-output plus buck-input nominal capacitance is about10.2 µF, but converter startup, its44 µF output, carrier rail/module capacitors, DC bias, RF load and source impedance must be included in the measured startup. AP63205's nominal4 ms soft start does not by itself close this gate. Local VS must remain above the switch's maximum4 V restart threshold during cold start.

At364 mA, a deliberately mixed-condition comparison using PPTC room-temperature R1max, CJ3407's25°C0.087 Ω and switch hot limits changes fuse+FET+switch drop from about237 mV to323 mV: **about86 mV additional path loss**. It is not a guaranteed hot budget. TPS1H200's400 mΩ maximum is stated at150°C; its5 mA active-supply maximum uses VS13.5 V, IN5 V and0.5 A load. Original TPS22810's cited85 µA belongs to its specified low-voltage conditions. Added consumption and D14/D15, buck/dropout/inductor, D11 and the actual C3/C6 module path must be measured. Both modules' loaded nominal5 V cold start/RF/brownout behavior remains open. No reduced input specification is used to force a pass.

## Whole-chain protection bounds

The nominal5/7.5/9/13.6 V examples are retained as intended cases; OEM tolerances, currents and transients are unknown. SMF16A's26 V maximum clamp is specified at7.7 A,10/1000 µs and25°C. Actual temperature, pulse shape, wiring overshoot, energy and repetition remain unqualified. TPS1H200's40 V rating addresses the former switch-rating mismatch in that bounded positive-pulse comparison; it does not confer40 V on the carrier.

Retained CJ3407 requires |VDS|<30 V and |VGS|<20 V with margin; AP63205 must stay within its32 V operating maximum, not target its35 V absolute rating. Both33 V PPTCs need their own differential-voltage bound; the downstream clamp does not bound raw connector voltage or voltage across a tripped fuse. High-node50 V capacitors do not resolve those weaker parts. A charged26 V protected node followed by−13.6 V at the PMOS drain creates39.6 V differential, beyond the retained PMOS. Reverse-step behavior therefore remains a qualification gate, not a claimed OEM event.

Switch CL does not limit upstream TVS current. PPTC maximum0.20 s trip at8 A does not establish that a200 W10/1000 µs SMF survives sustained overvoltage until trip. Bound source impedance/current, fault duration/energy and recovery. Littelfuse0.75 A hold/1.5 A trip are at20°C; reference hold values0.47 A at70°C and0.36 A at85°C are not an enclosed-board source allowance. D14/D15's current/thermal limits remain independent;0.606 A CL is not a new continuous diode/system rating.

## TI package rule and assembly gates

The custom U9/U10 footprint follows current **TI Rev E May2026, DGN0008K-C01 pp28–29**: lead pads1.4 ×0.45 mm, row spacing4.4 mm and pitch0.65 mm. Thus adjacent copper-pad gap is0.20 mm. Two explicit rules apply that minimum only to pairs of pads within the same named U9 or U10 footprint. Global0.20 mm and Power0.25 mm clearance, routes, vias and other footprints retain their original rules. Short0.20 mm track necks escape the fine-pitch pads under those unchanged routing rules.

This is the manufacturer's intended intrinsic package geometry and an engineering exception to the Power pad rule. JLC publishes a0.15 mm minimum different-net SMD pad gap at1 oz, so the0.20 mm geometry is fabricable. **No universal insulation/IPC/environmental compliance is claimed.** Contamination, coating, operating environment and assembler acceptance remain open.

EP copper is2 ×3 mm, mask-defined exposed area1.57 ×1.89 mm, and one matching full paste aperture follows TI's0.125 mm stencil example. Optional under-paste vias are omitted; external0.8/0.4 mm GND vias outside the exposed/paste pad connect to the ground plane. TI p28 explicitly makes vias optional. Final ≥85% solder coverage, stencil/process approval and actual thermal performance still require qualification. The JEDEC47.4°C/W is not a prediction for this layout. New STEP files are explicitly approximate maximum package envelopes derived from TI/Littelfuse drawings, not authenticated vendor CAD.
