# Rev 3J case, 11 mm nominal module stack

Use matching-base with either the C3 or C6 closed-USB lid and the three light pipes. STEP is the editable solid and STL is the printable mesh. The exterior remains 105.13 × 54 mm to preserve the validated shell joint, spring features and both button locations. The board is 90.13 × 37.94 mm. Aligning on the socket rows keeps the USB receptacle, switches and LEDs in their original locations. The posts, mating relief, jack opening, tail pockets and guide clearances follow the new board. Run generate.py with Python and build123d 0.10.0 to reproduce the parts. All dimensions and ray checks are in case-checks.json.

The base and closed lid have zero CAD overlap with the nominal board solid. Every J7 and JP3 pin has 3.50 mm below the board, the XIAO socket pins have 2.40 mm and the eight RJ45 contacts have 3.00 mm. Twenty rays across the USB mouth on each lid encountered at least 2.03 mm of wall. Fit the fully assembled carrier, connector, socket tails, cable plug, buttons and light pipes in a printed prototype before qualification. Case closure supports the disconnect-before-USB instruction. The printed enclosure provides no electrical isolation.
