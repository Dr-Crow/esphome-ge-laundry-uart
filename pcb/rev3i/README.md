## Board and case
The board went from 99 × 40 mm to 90.13165 × 40 mm, an 8.96% area reduction. Sections and interconnecting routes were moved. The buck switch-node and bootstrap tracks retain their original local geometry, and the RF keepout stays fixed to the module. Both protected inputs, priority selection, XIAO sockets, service links, probe pads and the listen-only J7 monitor are present. The closed USB lids remain part of the prototype protection strategy. Keep the PC disconnected with an appliance attached. The monitor header shares circuit ground. Use an isolated fixture for debug. Do not bridge an input protection device or connect a PC UART directly to an unverified appliance signal or ground.

The previously named Rev 3H-C has been promoted to Rev 3I. The circuit, component placements and routes are identical. The revision identifiers, silkscreen and resulting CAM were regenerated. The 88.65 mm trial failed copper clearance and silkscreen checks. The delivered width is the smallest clean routed trial in this study. The base is 105.13 mm long, down from 114 mm. The matching C3 and C6 closed lids have zero CAD overlap with the base and relocated J7/JP3 pin pockets have 3.50 mm of modeled free depth. Actual insertion, probe access, tail length, antenna routing and printed case fit still need a dry fit.

KiCad 9.0.9 reports zero ERC and DRC violations, zero unconnected items and zero schematic mismatches with all severities enabled. The comparison matched 59 logical nets, 98 footprint references, every pad net, and 680 routed item identifiers with their original layers, widths and nets. CAM retains four copper layers, 178 top paste objects, zero bottom paste objects, 135 plated holes and four nonplated holes. See the native reports, CAM plots and assembly lists.

## C9 and supply behavior
C9 changes from FH C303950 to Samsung CL31A106KBHNNNE, JLC C13585 (Basic), 10 µF, 50 V, 1206, X5R. C10 remains the independent 100 nF ceramic bypass. X5R has an 85 °C upper category temperature. Samsung's typical room-temperature bias curve gives 3.386 µF at 12 V and 2.548 µF at 15 V. The old part's operating-bias curve is unverified. Diodes' AP63205 evaluation board uses the same nominal 10 µF value, 1206 size and a separate 100 nF capacitor and lists X5R or X7R. [Samsung characteristic data](https://product.samsungsem.com/mlcc/CL31A106KBHNNN.do) · [Diodes evaluation board guide, BOM](https://www.diodes.com/assets/Evaluation-Boards/AP63205WU-EVM-User-Guide.pdf) · [Diodes regulator datasheet](https://www.diodes.com/assets/Datasheets/AP63200-AP63201-AP63203-AP63205.pdf) · [JLC part](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C13585)

The time-domain input-network model steps rail load from 0.10 to 0.60 A. It includes source R/L, an assumed 0.8 µF pre-eFuse capacitor, 28 mΩ fully enabled eFuse, the main compact inner power trace, C9 bias and C10. It uses idealized buck input pulses with duty 5/V. There are 216 cases at 12 and 15 V, sweeping 0.15–1 Ω and 0.25–3 µH of source impedance, 0.9/1.1 MHz and 65–120% of typical biased capacitance. Those multipliers test sensitivity and have no guaranteed tolerance meaning. At 0.5 Ω, 1 µH and 1.1 MHz, thinner copper gave 32/37 mV peak-to-peak settled ripple at the regulator input (12/15 V). The worst modeled values were 56/63 mV. The respective minimum input voltages across the sweep were 11.65/14.70 V. See the Python solver and input-transient.csv.

A separate 36-case 4.3 V dropout screen assumed the switch stayed on. At 0.60 A and 1 Ω, the 0.5 oz case reached about 3.66 V at the buck, below its 3.8 V operating minimum. The 1 oz option also went below 3.8 V. Even with an ideal harness this buck cannot generate regulated 5 V from 4.3 V. The real C3/C6 can respond differently. The load step is a stress case, not a continuous XIAO rating. The models do not predict eFuse turn-on, clamp action, closed-loop regulation, capacitor ESL, hot-plug overshoot, module resets or actual harness behavior.

## Copper and remaining checks
The selected four-layer 1.6 mm stack models 35 µm outer and 15.2 µm inner copper. The 1 oz comparison uses 30.48 µm inner copper. Confirm the as-built thickness and width tolerances with the factory. [JLCPCB stackups, JLC04161H-7628](https://jlcpcb.com/impedance)

The 27.05 mm main VDC inner segment adds 9.2 mV at 0.60 A versus thicker copper. The 21.12 mm inner 5 V path adds 7.9 mV at the same current. These are separate sides of a converter. The 3.3 V copper is branched, and the narrow buck feedback route carries sense current. The fine-grid DC mesh using the actual ground copper gives 1.39/1.92 mΩ from J1.8 to J6.6, about 0.32 mV additional drop at 0.60 A. The mesh assumes 20 µm plated via walls. Copper thickness, current redistribution, local temperature and high-frequency return behavior need real measurements. R34/R41 still set about 1.01 A nominal current limit on each input. The simulation's 0.60 A operating case does not establish fault-temperature margin.

On a current-limited, isolated fixture, exercise both input paths separately and together, check switchover and reverse current with each XIAO, and scope raw input, C9, module 5 V and 3.3 V through startup, Wi-Fi bursts and representative cable hot-plug. Check the eFuse thresholds and clamp overshoot against each part's rating. Check the lowest real appliance voltage and source impedance, module resets, maximum local C9 temperature, and protection-device/rail heating. Verify USB backfeed in both directions on the isolated fixture before considering any simultaneous-use claim. No powered test, order or vendor upload was made.

[Pololu's measured hot-plug waveforms](https://www.pololu.com/docs/0J16/all) approached 40 V on a 12 V supply with a 36-inch cable in their fixture. [Analog Devices AN88](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/application-notes/an88f.pdf) explains cable inductance and low-ESR ceramic ringing. A [Reddit buck-layout review](https://www.reddit.com/r/PrintedCircuitBoard/comments/1voiv09/review_request_tps54202based_33vregulator/) discusses keeping the capacitor and high-current switching loop compact on another regulator. The vendor design guide and measurements are the technical basis here.
