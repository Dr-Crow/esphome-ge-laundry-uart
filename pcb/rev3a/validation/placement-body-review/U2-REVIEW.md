This is an exact drawing/Fab/pad transform agreement; the printed pin1 and18 numbering on the top-view recommended land pattern fixes handedness.

The native footprint/CPL origin cannot automatically be called a geometric component center. A supplier's placement-library origin, packagezero rotation, pin1, pick datum and reel orientation must be matched to these typed facts before assembly placement approval. No source/CPL correction was made or inferred solely from body bounds. The source preserves the original intended body/terminal datum, rather than establishing JLC's library transform.
