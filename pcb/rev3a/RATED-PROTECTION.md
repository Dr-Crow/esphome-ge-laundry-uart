## Current limit, retry and supply margin

Nominal CL is 2000/3300=0.606 A. TI's conditional ±15% combined with ±1% resistor gives about 0.510–0.704 A, requiring VS−OUT≥2.5 V and the stated setting/test conditions. This is a provisional engineering budget, not an OEM allowance or unconditional normal draw ceiling. At 85 °C the resistor's±100 ppm/K reference broadens the partial estimate to about 0.507–0.708 A; source allowance, copper, diode, fuse and module limits remain separate.

Continued limit produces35–45 ms ON/0.8–1.2 s OFF, 40 ms/1 s typical, specified by design with thermal interaction. The old CT ramp is not an equivalent function. Unloaded0.510 A×35 ms≈17.9 mC arithmetic and AP63205's typical 4 ms soft start do not prove loaded startup. Local VS must remain above the maximum 4 V restart threshold. Startup, foldback, delayed EN, RF peaks, both handoffs and all eight GE/USB states require measurement.

The selected-part mixed-condition illustration adds about 86 mV path loss at 364 mA versus the former fuse/FET/switch choice. This reduces headroom when the buck is in dropout; it does not automatically reduce a regulated3.3 V rail by 86 mV. The conditional 5 mA switch-bias reference versus 85 µA adds 4.915 mA upstream of VS; using pre-VS fuse+PMOS0.487 Ω gives 2.394 mV additional loss, not switch-channel resistance or a direct subtraction from OUT CL. These references are not matched guaranteed corners.

Rev3A's AP63205→D11→AP2112K path is evaluated separately in [POWER-QUALIFICATION.md](POWER-QUALIFICATION.md).
