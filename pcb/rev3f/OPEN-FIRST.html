Assembly decision

C1, C3–C7, C12 and C14 bypass the regulated +3.3 V or +5 V rails. All eight now use the YAGEO CC0603KRX7R9BB104 already specified for C10/C15. Each site received the corresponding 0603 lands, paste, mask, fabrication outline and 3D model at its original centroid and rotation. Rev 3E used a 100 V Samsung 0805. The YAGEO is rated 50 V, 100 nF ±10%, X7R over −55 to +125 °C. Its typical low-bias curve is close to nominal at 3.3–5 V. The model does not guarantee minimum effective capacitance. Preserve the input, bootstrap and bulk requirements at their own sites. Verify local rail ripple with each module. YAGEO characteristic curves · original Samsung device.

Power path and copper decision

PIN1 travels from J1.1 on VDC through In2 to U9. PIN3 leaves J1.3 on ALT_PWR and uses front and back copper to U10. Both inputs have local capacitors and TVSs. The eFuse outputs join V_INPUT on the front layer. U8, L1 and C16/C17 generate BUCK_5V. Load current passes through D11 and R47 to +5 V, then through the distribution network to J6.7, the XIAO’s regulator and its +3.3 V output at J6.5. The 0.20 mm BUCK_5V run on In2 senses voltage at U8 FB. It does not carry XIAO load current. USB remains on the module. D11 protects the buck and the eFuses block reverse power toward the input pins. Appliance power can still reach host VBUS, and USB power can bias the GEA lines through the pull-ups and U11. Physically unplug the RJ45 before USB.

Rev 3F retains all through vias from the quote. It changes 16 existing In2 segments and adds one 0.60 mm diameter, 0.30 mm drill GND via at (87.9, 26.9) mm. The via stitches the local C6/D6 ground region after the smaller capacitor lands changed the fill. VDC uses the five verified shifted 1.00 mm segments. The XIAO +5 V feed uses 14.75 mm at 1.00 mm, 7.54 mm at 0.80 mm between LED vias and a 0.65 mm exit at 0.72 mm. +3.3 V branches use 0.75–1.00 mm. A uniform 1.00 mm conversion of those branches failed clearances. The final design uses the original clearance rules. Exact coordinate and width changes · net and layer inventory.

Open bench and factory gates

  • Startup, priority and dropout. PIN1’s 360 kΩ/150 kΩ UVLO divider screens to 4.254 V maximum rising in the inherited model. At a real 4.3 V J1 input this leaves 46 mV before local sense/harness sag. PIN3 uses a different EN pull-up; do not apply PIN1’s threshold to it. Q1 and the two OVLO dividers set the priority override and overvoltage behavior. A buck cannot regulate 5 V from 4.3 V. Check each input separately, both arrival/removal orders, post-D11 module input and the actual 3.3 V rail during boot and radio bursts. AP63205 full-load thermal guidance also depends on outer copper. Buck datasheet.
  • Bench instruction corrected. Removing R38 leaves U10 EN floating. Keep R38 fitted and use one connected isolated source for single-input tests. FLT at TP17/18 is open-drain and needs an appropriate external pull-up for measurement. TP13/14 expose priority and OVLO; probe with high impedance. TP15/16 are the local returns. R47 measures the total post-D11 load. C30 stays DNP. Revised schematic notes identify the actual Fig. 8-13 mux and U11 bus clamp.
  • Protection. The raw-input TVSs are roughly 35 mm from the jack pins. Their 27.6 V clamp test point is close to the eFuse 28 V absolute maximum. Harness/return inductance and hot-plug surge are unresolved. A reversed input can forward-bias a raw TVS upstream of either eFuse. Verify polarity before applying power. Use isolated, current-limited dummy sources, measure at each eFuse IN and buck VIN, and capture handoff, reverse-current floor, sag, heat and communications on both C3 and C6. No powered tests were performed. Fixture, measurements and stop criteria.
  • Stencil and assembly. U9/U10 retain the same copper lands, pin 1 orientation and top-side 0° placement. Each long IN/OUT land now has two nominal 0.28 × 1.06 mm rounded paste windows, a 0.20 mm internal web and ~82% coverage. Eight windows were measured in CAM. TI’s example uses a 0.10 mm stencil. Confirm the factory-generated stencil, thickness, corner apertures, mask webs, placement preview and buried-joint inspection/X-ray. Confirm paste treatment for the empty D8/C30 footprints. No X-ray charge or result is assumed. Window measurements.
  • Mechanical and RF. Preserve the 99 × 40 mm edge centerline, two mount holes, two jack peg holes, LED centers, J1 datum and both 8.5 mm HCTL sockets at 15.24 mm row separation. All 14 socket holes remain Ø1.02 mm. The modeled worst bare-tail case margin is only 0.55 mm before solder and print tolerance. At 1.44 mm board thickness it would lose another 0.16 mm. Verify solder-tail clearance, seating, both module-specific lids, button travel, full USB-C plug insertion, C3 antenna cable and C6 antenna performance. The four-layer keepout boundary and flags match the quote. Factory tooling must clear all copper and enclosure features. Mechanical review.

What was verified

Both source sets passed KiCad CLI 9.0.9 full-severity ERC and all-track DRC with schematic parity. Each has zero errors, warnings, exclusions, unconnected items and parity findings. Final 3F zones were refilled in the matching 9.0.9.1 board engine. The earlier layout iteration used 9.0.2. All 55 net names and 216 pin memberships match. All 93 footprint origins, rotations and sides match, including unpopulated features. The 69 fully assembled references generate a byte-identical CPL. ERC · DRC and parity · Netlist/BOM/CPL.

Fresh baseline plots matched every quoted Gerber’s vector rendering and both drill sets. Candidate plots contain all four copper layers, mask, paste, silk, outline and both drills. At 30 pixels/mm the intended front lands and silk, inner power routing, back probe labels, and front stencil account for the visible changes. One additional GND drill gives 122 PTH hits versus 121; the four NPTH hits match exactly. The added GND flash lands inside contiguous GND copper on In1/B.Cu, so object counts and painted-layer area can differ. Check the exact factory compensation before release.
