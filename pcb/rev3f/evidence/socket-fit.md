## First article: release gates

1. Get a matched pair from the same exact lot (HCTL baseline); keep candidate substitutes separate. On the calculator/bench record actual PCB thickness and finished-hole fit, body length/width/height, pin square diagonal and coplanarity; dry fit both rows before wave. No force, pin bending, housing overhang into other features, or rocking should be needed.
2. After assembly, measure peak tail **including** solder, base pocket depth on the actual print, and seat standoff with the board installed. Verify positive clearance at all 14 locations in the printed case; board must sit on both mount lands without deflection. Record actual clearance rather than assigning an unvalidated allowance; no trimming live/assembled hardware as a workaround.
3. With no power, mate the intended factory-pinned C3 and/or C6; verify keyed orientation, contact continuity on all 14 positions, no shorts, repeatable full seat and reasonable insertion/retention with no intermittent contacts on gentle movement. Measure supported fully seated stack, check against 10.35–11.65 mm screen, then check correct lid, buttons released/actuated, full USB insertion with intended plug, and antenna/light-guide clearance. If outside the endpoint interval, rerun enclosure checks at that actual stack before assuming it fits.
4. Resolve manufacturer drawing and assembler acceptance for any alternate, then repeat the same tests with that lot. Inspect populated Rev 3E new parts/clearance in that fit test. Powered or hotplug electrical tests are outside this mechanical note; follow the existing isolated bench handoff.
