USB and appliance-side service

The XIAO socket 5 V connection reaches the module’s USB supply path. D11 protects the upstream buck from the downstream rail. A diode in that position cannot disconnect the host’s USB VBUS or ground at the module. A VBUS-blocked cable still shares ground. With JP4 open the bus still has bias and ESD paths. None of these changes qualifies an ordinary laptop, USB-UART adapter or earth-grounded oscilloscope for a live appliance. Seeed C3 schematic · Seeed C6 schematic

Oven access is model-specific. The eight-position jack is a service connector. Before making an oven cable, identify the exact model, its service connector pinout and isolation classification, bus mode, idle levels, polarity and protocol. For live capture, use an appropriately rated, galvanically isolated service interface compatible with that port and its device-side power. Verify it on a dummy fixture first. A correctly matched detachable harness is preferable to modifying the oven or bypassing the carrier’s protected interface.

For receive-only diagnostics, the first bench setup should use a pre-opened JP4 board and a GEA3-only dummy harness that leaves the GEA2 line disconnected. GEA2 is a shared transmit/receive line. Removing a GPIO selection bridge alone does not guarantee a released bus. A separate GEA2 driver disconnect remains an option for a later board.

Closed-port case

Choose the closed-usb-lid-c3 or closed-usb-lid-c6 with matching-base in case/parts. Both STL and STEP are included. These use the recessed-end envelope, internal antenna configuration and 11.0 mm modeled stack. The C3 lid has FPC retention, the C6 lid a smooth antenna roof. Other enclosure variants and stack heights need their own fit check. The wall blocked all 20 sampled approach lines per model with 2.032 mm of material. Added wall material cleared the modeled connector box on both. The C6 parent lid already intersects the conservative box by 2.94 mm³ near its upper edge. Verify the actual C6 socket and button travel before accepting the print. Check the base, cable seating, shunt tails, LED guides, ventilation/temperature and antenna fit on physical articles. Validation and first articles

KiCad 9.0.9 full-severity ERC and DRC, schematic parity and unconnected-item checks are clear. The exported closed-link netlist has the same original component pin memberships as 3F after accounting for the R47 replacement and JP4 split. All 68 retained BOM/CPL rows match 3F exactly. Four service holes, the two JP3 holes and a replacement signal via are present in the drill comparison. Outline and non-plated drill hits match. In the exported copper, both pad pairs conduct by geometry and a simulated 0.30 mm gap separates each pair locally. These checks do not measure physical resistance, off-state leakage or host safety.
