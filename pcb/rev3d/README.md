# Rev3D candidate: FirstBuild-aligned eFuse power input

Rev3D is Rev3C with only the appliance power-input section replaced. The carrier is still 99 × 40 mm on
four layers, with the same XIAO C3/C6 sockets, RJ45, GEA2/GEA3 interface, AP63205 buck and all routing
outside the power section. It exists because three problems found in the Nov 2026 validation could not be
fixed by editing values alone:

1. **Out of stock:** U9/U10 TPS1H200AQDGNRQ1 (C2653785) and L1 ETQP3M4R7KVP (C412309) show zero stock at
   both LCSC and JLCPCB (checked live on 2026-11-22).
2. **No 5 V-pin margin:** the Rev3C chain (PPTC → PMOS → TPS1H200A → Schottky OR → buck → D11) drops about
   0.7–1.1 V at 0.5 A. A 5 V port pin left about 3.85 V at the XIAO 5V pin.
3. **PIN3 may be 5 V:** user reports give PIN3 as a 5 V rail on some appliances (mguaylam/esphome-gea#41,
   and the mulcmu washer schematic text). FirstBuild's reference adapter specifies "V_INPUT 4.3V - 15V".

## Reference design

FirstBuild's production adapter (github.com/geappliances/home-assistant-adapter) uses:

- the same XIAO ESP32-C3 on 1×7 headers
- one TPS22810 load switch per power pin, with transistor PIN1 priority
- OR diodes
- an AP2204K-5.0 LDO feeding the XIAO 5V pin

Rev3D keeps that topology (one switch per pin, PIN1 priority, a regulator into the XIAO 5V pin) and
removes the extra drops.

## What changed

| Function | Rev3C | Rev3D |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Per-pin protection and switch | 1812L075/33 PPTC + CJ3407 reverse PMOS + BZT52C12 gate clamps + TPS1H200A (0.6 A CL) | One **TPS259470A** eFuse (C3662799): true reverse blocking, −15 V tolerant, RON 28 mΩ, ILIM ≈1.0 A (R34/R41 3.3 kΩ; datasheet 0.85–1.15 A at 3.32 kΩ) |
| ORing | STPS140Z Schottkys D14/D15 | None needed: eFuse outputs tie together, reverse current is blocked |
| PIN1 priority | MMBT3904 senses PIN1 presence; a weak PIN1 could kill a healthy PIN3 | TI "Priority Power MUXing, Option 1" (SLVSFC9C Fig. 8-10): U9 AUXOFF drives U10 OVLO. AUXOFF asserts only once PIN1 is above UVLO (≈4.08 V rising / 3.71 V falling) and its inrush is complete |
| TVS | SMF16A (16 V standoff, 26 V clamp) | **SMF17A** (C2994190): 17 V standoff (clears the 16.2 V washer report), 27.6 V clamp at 7.2 A, below the eFuse 28 V abs max |
| Buck inductor | ETQP3M4R7KVP (OOS) | **Sunlord SWPA5040S4R7NT** (C305174): 4.7 µH, 30 mΩ, Isat 3.9 A, on the original pad centres |
| PIN1 overvoltage | None (TPS1H200A rated 40 V) | R44/R33 OVLO divider: lockout at ≈18.85 V rising / 17.1 V falling, which also hands over to PIN3. This keeps V_INPUT below U10's OUT abs max (VIN+21 V) when PIN3 is absent |
| Parts | 83 assembled refs, 34 unique, 19 extended | 68 assembled refs, 31 unique, 16 extended |

AP63205 behaviour near a 5 V input: datasheet "low dropout mode … effective duty cycle approximately
100%", with a 125 mΩ high-side switch. Estimated at 0.5 A from a 5.0 V source: about 4.9 V at BUCK_5V and
about 4.6 V at the XIAO 5V pin (FirstBuild's LDO chain gives about 4.3 V). This is a calculation only;
bench measurement is still required.

## Debug and rework provisions

| Provision | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| TP8 / TP9 | PIN1 / PIN3 at the connector side of each eFuse |
| TP10 / TP11 | eFuse ILM pins: analog current monitor per channel |
| TP3, TP1, TP2, TP7 | V_INPUT, +5V, +3V3, GND (unchanged from Rev3C) |
| Remove R31 (or R38) | Holds U9 (or U10) off, to test the other input alone |
| JP2 (3-pad solder jumper) | GEA3 module TX source. Default 1–2 = D6 (UART0, prints ROM boot text at reset). Cut and bridge 2–3 for D4 (C3 GPIO6 / C6 GPIO22, silent at boot), plus a firmware change |
| JP2 pads | Probe points for module TX (D6 / D4) and the buffer input |

DVdT, ITIMER and FLT are left open: TI's default of fastest slew and fastest overcurrent response. U10
EN uses a single 360 kΩ pull-up, as TI allows for >5 V supplies with ≥350 kΩ. U10 OVLO is used for
priority, so PIN3 has no overvoltage lockout. A sustained PIN3 level above ~21 V with PIN1 absent would
exceed U9's OUT abs max; reported PIN3 levels are 5–14 V. TI's fix for both inputs (SLVSFC9C Fig. 8-13)
needs an extra N-FET and is not fitted. The IN/OUT pad paste is reduced to ~81% (TI stencil ~82%).

## Verification status (digital only)

- **ERC:** native KiCad 9.0.9 at full severity, 0 errors / 0 warnings.
- **DRC:** all-track errors plus schematic parity, 0 violations, 0 unconnected, 0 parity differences.
  The custom `.kicad_dru` rule relaxes clearance to 0.19 mm only between items that both touch the U9/U10
  courtyards. TI's land pattern itself has 0.20 mm IN/OUT bar gaps.
- **Netlist:** 60 intended connections asserted pin by pin against the exported netlist (all pass).
- **BOM/CPL:** generated by `manufacturing/generate.py` from `kicad-cli` exports and checked by
  `ci/validate.py manufacturing`. J1 uses the unchanged EVERCOM body datum from Rev3C.
- **Live JLCPCB stock (2026-11-22):** every part in stock. Lowest is the existing 74LVC2G07 (513).
- **Independent review:** a separate design review (findings in the working notes) found no blockers. Its
  two majors are fixed here: the PIN1 OVLO divider and ILIM raised from 0.71 A to 1.0 A.

**Not done:** supplier quote and preview review, appliance pin measurements, bench power tests, thermal
and transient tests, enclosure fit. The release gates stay blocked until there is physical evidence.
