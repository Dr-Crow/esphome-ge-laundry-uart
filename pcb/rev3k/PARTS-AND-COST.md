# Parts and cost for five fully assembled carriers

**Keep the six remaining paid SMT part numbers.** The substitutions already in this BOM are Samsung C13585 at C9 and C12891 at C16/C17. The historical quote identified eight paid Extended SMT SKUs. Removing these two distinct codes predicts a six-feeder charge of **$18.54 per five-board order** at the historical $3.09 rate, a $6.18 reduction versus quoted 3E. Rev 3J already includes both changes. Savings from further component swaps in 3K are **$0**. Parts-library listings and feeder promotions can change. The original charge is vendor evidence, while the six-SKU charge is a model. ## What to quote

Use one design, five PCBs and assembly quantity five, lead-free, Economic, four layers, top placements only, all 72 references per carrier. Request the actual diagnostic connector and both sockets fitted. In the factory placement preview inspect J1's body origin, every polarized part, U9/U10 exposed pads and rotation, plus J5/J6/J7 insertion. That review checks assembly geometry. Startup and reverse-current performance need separate measurements.

The $94.22 Rev 3E quote was before shipping and tax. The $16.71 differential for 0.5 oz versus 1 oz inner copper was verified in the earlier bare-board comparison. Subtracting that differential and two $3.09 fees from $94.22 gives **$71.33 for five** if everything else stays at the old price. That arithmetic omits changes in component prices, added J7 labor, tooling and via process, which may outweigh a smaller outline. No per-area saving is included. Get independent line items for single-board tooling/rails, rail removal, any filled-and-capped vias, finish, insertion labor, stencil and freight. Disallow automatic omission of through-hole placements. Keep the 1 oz inner option priced with all other choices identical. ## Capacitor and copper limits

The Samsung CL31A106KBHNNNE is a 10 µF 50 V X5R, 1206, rated to 85 °C. Its typical data gives 3.386 µF at 12 V and 2.548 µF at 15 V. C10 remains alongside it. The regulator evaluation board uses a nominal 10 µF input capacitor and separate ceramic bypass. The Rev 3I simulation is useful for selecting harness/load corners, but does not validate the new power and ground geometry. Verify the installed temperature and check input ripple, supply margin and source impedance. [Samsung bias data](https://product.samsungsem.com/mlcc/CL31A106KBHNNN.do) · [Diodes evaluation board guide](https://www.diodes.com/assets/Evaluation-Boards/AP63205WU-EVM-User-Guide.pdf) · [Earlier model and assumptions](https://github.com/Dr-Crow/esphome-ge-laundry-uart/blob/c89435367d6d32aac23be7610cbfe3ad9f6273dc/pcb/rev3i/README.md)

The 0.5 oz option applies only to In1 and In2. The outer layers are 1 oz. The two 3.3 kΩ ILIM resistors still set about 1.01 A nominal per input. The 0.60 A operating case is a screening assumption, not the eFuse trip point. Obtain factory stack and minimum finished copper, via plating and etch/width tolerance. Check both raw inputs to the eFuses, both outputs to U8, the buck output through D11/JP3 to J6.7, J6.5 to the 3.3 V branch loads, and ground back to J1.8. Switching return inductance and heat at current limit need separate checks from steady-state copper resistance. [JLC copper guide](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/jlcpcb-copper-weight)

JLC’s laminated structure guide uses 15.2 µm finished inner copper for nominal 0.5 oz and 1.2 mil (30.48 µm) for nominal 1 oz in its reference calculations. These are design values. Its published trace-width tolerance is ±20%. A 20% narrower trace raises its resistance by 25% at the same temperature and thickness. Applying that factor to the entire 32.3 mV modeled JP3-to-XIAO drop at 0.60 A gives a conservative width-only screen of 40.4 mV. Via, contact, temperature and process variation need separate treatment. The public guide does not promise a finished minimum inner foil thickness for this individual order.
