## What changed

The jack, protected inputs, regulator cluster, module socket pair, monitor header, LEDs and mounting holes were repositioned and the interconnects rerouted. The XIAO C3/C6 socket spacing and RF exclusion move as one group. The switching-node and bootstrap tracks move with the buck cluster. Both protected appliance inputs, priority logic, D11, the cut-and-restore service links, 18 test points and the J7 listen-only header are retained. The new common base and two closed-USB lids measure 77.2 × 40.0 × 31.9 mm assembled. The physical jack mouth overhangs the edge by 0.38 mm. The nominal case has two integral printed button springs per lid and a friction closure. See case/README.md for antenna and dry-fit checks.

Native **KiCad 9.0.9** ERC and DRC with all severities and schematic parity report zero violations, zero opens and zero parity issues. Geometry editing used pcbnew 9.0.2. The 98 footprint references and 232 pad records match 3J by reference, pad and net. The exported BOM is byte-for-byte identical to 3J. Both CPLs contain the same 72 fitted references, all top-side. The fitted BOM contains 32 distinct LCSC codes. J1 uses the translated manufacturer body center for placement. The review CAM has four copper layers, 178 front paste objects, zero back paste, 225 plated and four nonplated drills. The extra small plated holes are ordinary through-vias. No drilled via overlaps a surface solder land or an exposed probe land on either face. All 192 vias are conventional through-vias with board-level tenting. See evidence/via-to-land.csv for the four fitted pads with a via annulus within 0.105 mm. The smallest fitted-pad copper gap is 0.092 mm. New service labels use 1.0 mm characters with 0.16 mm strokes. Get the fabricator's actual tenting, mask registration and mask opening signoff.

## Manufacturing and cost

**Review the edge clearance before committing to this outline.** The supplier specifies 2.5 mm between each component body and the board edge. Fifteen assembled references screen below this, including the intentional J1 overhang, the XIAO sockets at 2.183 mm using HCTL's maximum body, C6 at 0.65 mm and R31/R32 at 0.638 mm on the nominal package drawing. evidence/component-body-edge.csv covers every fitted reference. The jack and sockets use manufacturer body dimensions. Other rows use package fabrication drawings, which can include terminals and stroke width. Confirm their production body envelopes. Ask JLC which edges need breakaway tooling and how J1 will be inserted and depanelized. Get any tooling and removal priced separately. Both candidate sizes fit the published Economic outline range. Size eligibility alone does not settle assembly handling. ## Power, protection and service

At the 0.60 A screening point, the modeled voltage drops with 15.2 µm inner copper are 10.9 mV from J1.1 to the primary eFuse, 24.6 mV from J1.3 to the alternate eFuse and 32.3 mV from JP3.2 to the module 5 V pin. Changing the inner layers to 30.48 µm at the same geometry reduces the last two to 18.6 and 30.7 mV. The ground mesh from J1.8 to J6.6 gives 3.33 mV versus 1.89 mV. Copper-only results, other branches, source/sink pins and model assumptions are in evidence/. The R34/R41 limit remains about 1.01 A nominal per protected input. See the earlier C9 bias and low-line work in the component review. Half-ounce is a reasonable first quote option. Real current, voltage margin and temperature remain gates.

Unplug the appliance cable before using USB. The two closed lids physically restrict USB access when assembled. D11 controls the feed toward the module, but the XIAO implementation and common ground still require a C3 and C6 backfeed test. J7 is a **3.3 V listen-only** service point. Use an isolated fixture with the receiver at pin 2 for GEA2, pin 3 for GEA3 appliance-to-module, or pin 4 for GEA3 module-to-appliance. Pin 1 is ground. Each signal has a series 1 kΩ resistor. Leave the adapter TX and supply disconnected. Do not connect a grounded PC directly to an unverified appliance ground. To trace power, follow TP8/TP9 at the raw inputs, TP3 at the protected input, TP12 at the buck output, TP1 at 5 V and TP2 at 3.3 V. TP7, TP15 and TP16 are ground. JP3 isolates the protected carrier-to-module feed. JP4 isolates its documented signal section. Both links can be restored on their designated pads after a continuity check. The backside legend and evidence/debug-pad-map.csv identify the pads. Never bridge an eFuse or clamp as a service workaround.

Before ordering, close tooling, solder mask, 2D/3D placement, through-hole insertion, QFN stencil and orientation with the factory. Dry-fit both actual modules, antennas and a latched cable in a test print. Before any appliance connection, use an isolated current-limited fixture to check each input alone and together, switchover, reverse current, fault indication, C9 and rail transients during radio bursts, clamp overshoot, local temperature and USB backfeed in each direction. No powered test was performed.
