# Corrective review for compact GE appliance adapters

**If funding one electrical first article, take 3K-C2 to factory review first.** It keeps the diagnostic access and gives each eFuse its own local output capacitor. The smaller 3L-E tests the alternative of sharing one capacitor. The active K/L/M files remain available as the reviewed baseline. The compact boards remain experimental. Unplug the appliance harness before connecting USB. A clean KiCad report cannot establish behavior during hot-plug, a signal-driven unpowered board, a source fault or an enclosure fit test.

| Priority | Finding | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Factory placement data used pin 1 for the XIAO sockets and debug headers | Corrected eight CPL records across K/L/M. J5 and J6 moved 7.62 mm to the body center on every board. J7 moved 3.81 mm on K/L. Native regeneration checks 72, 72 and 68 top-side fitted placements. |
| 2 | The C3 antenna occupied part of a case closure tongue | Revised each common base and exported STEP and STL. A 9.000 mm³ collision became 0.350 mm of modeled clearance. All 12 base/lid checks against the C3 antenna and coax pass. The remaining 0.120 mm lid-to-antenna and 0.218 mm lid-to-coax clearances require a print and the real antenna. |
| 3 | U11 offers a path from powered interface pins to module 5 V, even with JP3 open | Corrected user guidance and retained the closed USB lid. Opening JP3 disconnects the buck feed only. Remove the appliance cable before USB use. Simultaneous operation remains unsupported on K/L/M. |
| 4 | U9 and U10 lack local output decoupling | Both outputs join V_INPUT. C9 sits at the buck, and C20/C21 decouple the two inputs. Keep C9. Two separately marked native prototypes implement protection changes. 3K-C2 adds one capacitor per eFuse. 3L-E adds one shared capacitor. See the comparison below before choosing a fabrication package. |
| 5 | GEA3 receive thresholds and transmit rise time depend on the appliance and its harness | Added a reproducible native-netlist screen. At the nominal modeled 200 pF load without an external pull-up, J1 pin 5 reaches 3.5 V in 2.649 µs. Half a bit is 2.170 µs. A stiff 3.30 V appliance high at J1 pin 4 gives 3.308 V at U4, below its guaranteed 3.50 V high at 5.00 V. The proposed change to power U4 at 3.3 V also weakens GEA2 low margin and was rejected. |
| 6 | The printed QR code led users toward Rev 2 programming instructions | The public repository now directs compact-board owners to the four C3/C6 and GEA2/GEA3 profiles. The guide identifies the correct dependency pin, fixed 3M routing, J7 listen-only use and USB precautions. |

Both electrical trials retain C9 and reuse its Samsung CL31A106KBHNNNE (C13585) for the extra placements. Each still uses 32 distinct order codes. Across five assembled carriers, 3K-C2 adds ten capacitor placements, 3L-E adds five. Their prices and tooling charges need new vendor quotes. Samsung’s typical 25 °C curve gives about 2.55 µF at 15 V and 1.79 µF at the 19.67 V upper-threshold screening point. Installed effective capacitance at bias and temperature must satisfy the transient design. The original 1 oz reference and the previously modeled half-ounce stack do not establish the finished copper, waveform or temperature of a new board. [Samsung characteristics](https://product.samsungsem.com/mlcc/CL31A106KBHNNN.do) · [TI TPS25947](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf)

Each experimental board passed KiCad 9.0.9 ERC, all-severity DRC and schematic parity, and has a separately marked physical revision. Inspect its own evidence and use its matching manufacturing files. The corrected enclosures retain the parent board outlines. Actual populated clearance, printed fit and factory acceptance remain open.

### Evidence for the fixes

- The generator now checks corrected centers against the reviewed body dimensions. Native regeneration reproduced each BOM and CPL byte for byte. All 42 CAM members matched after normalization of creation timestamps. JLC still needs to confirm actual placement, part orientation, through-hole operations and any edge tooling. [Placement evidence](rev3m/evidence/connector-centroid-review.md)
- Corrected bases retain four closure catches. The modeled interference used for retention fell from 0.420 to 0.355 mm³ per assembled lid. Measure closing force, C3 lead/antenna fit, C6 button rest and actuation, light-guide retention and RF performance on real parts. [Case fit and measurements](rev3k/case/PRINT-FIT.md)
- The J1 data pins connect to U11 before the series resistors. Its upper steering diodes reach the module-side +5 V node shared with J6.7. The carrier also has pull-ups on the signaling paths. An open JP3 or D11 in the buck path cannot isolate these paths or the common appliance ground. [ProTek SRV05-4](https://protekdevices.com/products/series-details/?id=5150) · [Service and firmware guide](../firmware/shared-rev3c/README.md)
- One candidate for a later board is TI TPD4E1U06DBVR, which has line-to-ground protection and an internally unconnected pin 5. It would eliminate the direct upper-diode feed to +5 V but would also remove the original array’s dedicated rail clamp and has a lower specified surge capability. J1 pins 4, 5 and 7 would still have resistive paths back to +5 V. Preserve U11 on the active boards pending a system transient requirement and comparison of package lands, rail protection and clamp voltage at the protected devices. This is an electrical option with no confirmed cost saving. The [protection comparison](compact-review/DATA-LINE-PROTECTION.md) includes the independent clamp alternatives, exact pins, surge ratings and remaining pull-up paths. The baseline KiCad U11 datasheet field points to Semtech; identify the fitted part by ProTek C85364. [TI TPD4E1U06](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tpd4e1u06.pdf)
- The native GEA3 screen verifies the resistor values, buffer rails, JP4 connections and the connector pins before it solves the loaded line. It records the trade-off between a stronger pull-up, rise time and the remote low level. [Model, assumptions and results](compact-review/GEA3-HARNESS.md)
- An independent KiCad 9.0.9 rerun on all three active carriers found zero ERC, DRC, unrouted and schematic-parity findings. The receipt gives both design hashes for each board. [Native recheck](compact-review/native-recheck.json)

### First-article review order

1. **Before purchasing**, review J1, J5, J6 and, where fitted, J7 on the factory's actual placement overlay. Check jack overhang and handling, socket pin 1, all through-hole orientations and the underside QR on the actual production layers. Request a fresh lead-free Economic quote with factory marking off and compare extra tooling separately. Retain mask/silkscreen contrast. A legacy reorder may retain factory marking. [JLC marking policy](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/how-to-mark-on-pcb)
2. **Before applying power**, dry-fit the printed case with the selected pre-headered module, actual antenna/coax, light guides and latching cable. Verify C6 buttons return freely, the enclosure keeps USB covered when closed and the physical QR scans.
3. **On an isolated current-limited low-voltage fixture**, check the unpowered carrier with each interface line driven separately by a representative source. Measure line current, J6.7 and J6.5 and the module USB VBUS pad with the host absent. Repeat for C3 and C6 with JP3 in each state. Record instrument sensitivity, the actual module version and measured limits. Keep the production use rule of physically separating host USB and the appliance harness.
4. **Verify both protected inputs**, pin-1 priority, interruption, return of the primary source and behavior when the selected source remains in range but overloads. For 3K-C2, Allow underside fixture clearance for TP14 (/PIN3_OV) and TP18 (/PIN3_FLT_N). Probe-tip clearance on a populated board remains untested. TP13 (/AUXOFF1), TP17 (/PIN1_FLT_N), TP3 (/V_INPUT), TP8 (VDC) and TP9 (/ALT_PWR) have exposed top lands. Monitor both local OUT pins relative to their local returns, and the buck input, 5 V and 3.3 V. Cross-check startup, radio-load steps, fault interruption, negative excursions and handoff with the installed capacitors. TI’s −0.8 V OUT rating applies only to pulses shorter than 1 µs. Use a design margin inside the applicable rating. R34 and R41 set about 1.01 A nominal. Do not use the earlier 0.60 A DC model as the fault limit. [TI TPS25947, ratings and layout recommendations](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf)
5. **Verify GEA2 and GEA3** on both module variants. Record idle and reset traffic and high/low timing at the appliance connector with the actual harness. Keep J7 as a module-side receive-only monitor during testing.

The board outlines, part counts and assembly side in the K/L/M comparison are unchanged by the connector and case corrections. There is no new vendor quote, physical print result or powered test result for this correction pass.
